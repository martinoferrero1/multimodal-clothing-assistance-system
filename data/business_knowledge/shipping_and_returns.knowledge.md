# Shipping and Returns

## Shipping Policy

We offer multiple shipping options depending on your location and urgency.

### Shipping Methods

- Standard Shipping: 3 to 5 business days
- Express Shipping: 1 to 2 business days

Delivery times may vary due to:
- Location
- Weather conditions
- High-demand periods (e.g., sales, holidays)

### Order Processing

- Orders are typically processed within 24 to 48 hours
- Orders placed on weekends or holidays may be delayed

### Tracking

Once your order is shipped:
- You will receive a tracking number via email
- You can monitor the delivery status in real time

---

## Returns Policy

We want you to be fully satisfied with your purchase.

### Return Conditions

- Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery
- Items must be:
  - Unused
  - In original condition
  - With tags and packaging intact

### Non-returnable Items

Some items may not be eligible for returns, including:
- Personal care products (if opened)
- Innerwear (for hygiene reasons)
- Final sale items

### Refunds

- Refunds are processed after inspection of returned items
- The refund will be issued to the original payment method
- Processing time may take 5 to 10 business days
