# Store Overview

Our store specializes in a wide range of fashion products designed for different styles, occasions, and seasons.

## Product Categories

We offer products across the following main categories:

### Apparel
Includes a variety of clothing items such as:
- Topwear: T-shirts, shirts, sweaters, jackets, sweatshirts
- Bottomwear: Jeans, trousers, shorts, skirts, leggings
- Dresses and jumpsuits
- Innerwear and loungewear
- Seasonal clothing (e.g., jackets, swimwear)

### Footwear
Our footwear selection includes:
- Casual shoes
- Formal shoes
- Sports shoes
- Sandals and flip flops
- Heels and flats

### Accessories
We provide a broad range of accessories such as:
- Bags (backpacks, handbags, laptop bags, wallets)
- Jewellery (necklaces, bracelets, rings)
- Watches
- Sunglasses
- Belts, scarves, and hats

### Personal Care
Includes beauty and grooming products:
- Skincare products
- Makeup items
- Fragrances
- Hair care products

## Product Availability

Product availability may vary depending on:
- Season
- Stock levels
- Ongoing promotions

We continuously update our catalog to reflect the latest trends and seasonal collections.
