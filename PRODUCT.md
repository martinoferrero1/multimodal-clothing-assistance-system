# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Lookeate serves clients and stores. Clients explore garments and outfits, develop
their personal style, and create pieces. Stores provide the catalog and business
context that can support garment discovery and customer questions.

## Product Purpose

Lookeate brings complementary ways to discover, create, and combine personal
style into one platform. It aims to support both AI-assisted exploration and
hands-on creativity, with a longer-term vision of connecting people who use
Lookeate ("Lookeaters").

## Positioning

Lookeate combines AI-assisted garment and outfit search and creation with tools
for designing pieces and a vision for connection among Lookeaters. These
experiences are intended to complement one another rather than force every task
through a single interface.

## Operating Context

The authenticated home brings the product experiences together. Lookeate
Assistant supports natural-language and image-assisted conversations about
garments, outfits, and store information. Create your style lets users compose
an outfit manually on a rotatable mannequin.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Lookeate is in beta. Lookeate Assistant and the base manual style creator are
  available; garment creation and structured catalog exploration are upcoming.
- The current manual style creator does not persist looks. Saved looks, richer
  garment design, and social connections are product directions, not claims of
  currently available functionality.
- Assistant recommendations use catalog and business information, and users can
  provide reference images and style preferences.
- The specific needs of stores, the social workflows, and the primary user
  scenarios within each audience remain open product decisions.

## Brand Commitments

The public application is **Lookeate**; its conversational styling module is
**Lookeate Assistant**. People using Lookeate may be called Lookeaters. Beta
describes the product as a whole.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains a working web application, catalog data in
`data/clothes.csv`, and business knowledge documents in
`data/business_knowledge/`. The README describes current capabilities and
planned work. Do not present planned experiences or unverified customer outcomes
as established facts.

## Product Principles

- Give clients complementary ways to explore and shape their style.
- Combine AI assistance with direct creative control.
- Connect recommendations to relevant catalog and store information.
- Make the availability of each experience clear as the beta evolves.
- Treat community connection as a product direction until its workflows exist.
